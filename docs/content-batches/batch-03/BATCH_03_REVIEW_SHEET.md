## High-risk checks

### Kids
- Is the narrative concrete, sequential, and non-graphic?
- Does the lesson avoid private family, harm, grief, or fear disclosures?
- Are promise, providence, rescue, and forgiveness stated without guarantees the passage does not make?

### Youths
- Are timeline, covenant order, cause/effect, and text-versus-inference skills visible?
- Is abstraction anchored to a path, grid, sequence, or cycle?
- Are fear, judgment, oppression, and violence handled without shaming or simplistic formulas?

### Teens
- Are explicit text, canonical synthesis, disputed questions, historical context, and prudential application distinguishable?
- Is the strongest credible alternative represented accurately where relevant?
- Are flood scope, Israel/land continuity, typology, political analogy, and law taxonomy handled within stated evidence limits?

## Publication rule

No lesson should move from `in_review` to `approved` until all applicable review states are completed and all externally verifiable historical or scientific claims have source support suitable for publication.
