## Review priorities

- K1-L07: keep the promise specific to Abraham and Sarah; do not imply that every hoped-for child or outcome is guaranteed.
- K1-L08: providence must not excuse wrongdoing, force forgiveness, or require reunion with an unsafe person.
- K1-L09: distinguish God's attention and rescue purpose from a promise that every suffering situation ends immediately.
- K1-L10: keep the rescue central, avoid graphic death, and do not promise that every danger will end through a miracle.
- Y1-L07: hold rescue, remembrance, oppression, and severe judgment together without dehumanising Egyptians or trivialising moral difficulty.
- Y1-L08: preserve the rescue-before-law order and explain New Covenant application without copying every Sinai stipulation directly.
- Y1-L09: distinguish fear from the communal decision to reject God's promise; do not diagnose anxiety as unbelief.
- Y1-L10: teach the Judges cycle without turning every judge into a hero or every modern hardship into a direct sin-punishment formula.
- T1-L07: distinguish explicit narrative claims from disputed flood-scope and scientific questions; avoid disaster blame.
- T1-L08: distinguish Genesis promises, Paul's Christ-centred use, and unresolved Israel/land questions without political tests of faith.
- T1-L09: follow later canonical reuse of Exodus without uncontrolled typology or automatic political analogies.
- T1-L10: preserve rescue, covenant, holiness, mediation, and presence; source-review the later moral/civil/ceremonial law taxonomy before publication.

Schema validity is not evidence of theological accuracy or teaching effectiveness. T1-L07 requires careful treatment of flood scope and external scientific claims. T1-L08 requires review of Israel, land, Christ, and church continuity. T1-L09 requires discipline around typology and political application. T1-L10 requires source support for historical law-classification terminology.
