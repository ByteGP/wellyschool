## High-risk review checks

### Kids
- Are abstract ideas anchored to story action, objects, pictures, or sequence?
- Do provision, commandments, appearance, fear, and grief avoid shame and disclosure pressure?
- Is metaphor explained without flattening it into literal description?

### Youths
- Are timeline, genre, immediate context, cause, and inference visibly taught?
- Are authority and political applications kept secondary to the biblical context?
- Are outsider inclusion, providence, and covenant fulfilment stated without overreach?

### Teens
- Are event, theological interpretation, historical reconstruction, canonical synthesis, framework position, and disputed alternatives clearly separated?
- Are land, kingship, exile, and new-creation claims protected from nationalism and speculative timetables?
- Does the lesson permit honest lament and uncertainty while preserving the approved doctrinal core?

## Publication rule

No lesson moves from `in_review` to `approved` until required review states are complete and the rendered lesson has been tested in both public experiences.
