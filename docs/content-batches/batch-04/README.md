## Review priorities

- **K1-L11:** do not shame hunger or food insecurity; provision language must not promise identical miracles or blame families with unmet needs.
- **K1-L12:** keep rescue before instruction; do not teach obedience as earning salvation or unquestioning compliance with unsafe adults.
- **K1-L13:** distinguish visible appearance, observable actions, and the heart known fully by God; avoid body or popularity comparisons.
- **K1-L14:** treat Psalm 23 as poetry; acknowledge danger and grief rather than promising a life without loss.
- **Y1-L11:** distinguish explicit divine action from providence inference; do not turn bereavement, migration, or marriage into a reward formula.
- **Y1-L12:** retain legitimate leadership concerns, distrustful motives, and the warning about concentrated power; avoid partisan analogy.
- **Y1-L13:** preserve immediate dynastic fulfilment before later Christological synthesis; do not excuse failed leaders through covenant language.
- **Y1-L14:** trace multiple causes of division; evaluate counsel by content rather than adviser age and reject coercive leadership.
- **T1-L11:** distinguish land, information, temporary judges, covenant allegiance, and heart transformation; avoid modern territorial or authoritarian shortcuts.
- **T1-L12:** hold promise, discipline, historical collapse, lament, prophetic development, and resurrection fulfilment together.
- **T1-L13:** separate event, revealed covenant interpretation, external history, and modern speculation; do not blame victims of catastrophe.
- **T1-L14:** preserve original prophetic audiences, New Testament use, disputed Israel/church horizons, and the project’s non-timetable eschatology.
